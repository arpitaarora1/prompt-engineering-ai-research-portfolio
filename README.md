# Prompt Engineering & AI Research Portfolio

## 📌 About the Project

This repository contains my **BBA FinTech & AI Prompt Engineering and AI-Driven Research Portfolio**. It demonstrates how Generative AI can be used for business problem-solving, research planning, content generation, analysis, and source verification.

The portfolio includes practical business prompts and AI-generated responses across areas such as **Marketing, HR, Finance, Operations, and Business Research**.

## 📂 Project Contents

### 1. Foundational Prompt Engineering

Includes basic prompting techniques such as:

* Zero-shot prompting
* Few-shot prompting
* Marketing content generation
* Interview questions
* Employee motivation
* Financial analysis
* Business problem-solving

### 2. Advanced Prompt Engineering

Covers advanced techniques including:

* Constraint-based prompts
* Structured output prompts
* Prompt chaining
* Business analysis
* HR and operations problem-solving
* Product comparison

These prompts demonstrate how adding constraints, structure, and specific requirements can improve AI-generated outputs.

### 3. Business Prompt Engineering

Contains practical prompts for:

* Marketing
* Human Resources
* Finance
* Operations
* Customer retention
* Recruitment
* Business decision-making

The portfolio focuses on using AI to support practical business tasks and decision-making.

### 4. AI-Driven Research: Source Verification & Citations

This section focuses on responsible AI-assisted research, including:

* Converting broad topics into research questions
* Identifying primary and secondary sources
* Evaluating source credibility
* Verifying statistics and claims
* Detecting fake citations
* Creating evidence tables
* Checking publication dates and data relevance

The project emphasizes that AI-generated information should be verified before being used as research evidence.

### 5. PhonePe Case Study

The PhonePe section demonstrates how AI tools were used as research assistants for studying:

* AI and fraud detection
* Credit risk
* Customer experience
* Business model and financial context
* Prompt refinement
* Source verification
* Critical evaluation of AI-generated information

The research process included comparing AI outputs with dated and named sources instead of accepting AI-generated claims at face value.

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Through this project, I learned how to:

* Write clear and effective prompts
* Use different prompt engineering techniques
* Apply AI to real-world business problems
* Structure AI outputs for business use
* Develop focused research questions
* Identify reliable sources
* Verify AI-generated claims and statistics
* Recognize outdated or unsupported information
* Use AI as a research assistant while maintaining fact-checking practices
